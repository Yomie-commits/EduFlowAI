What it does

Four jobs your office does by hand, done for you

Admissions enquiries

One inbox for the website form, email, phone and walk-ins. Each parent gets a reference within minutes, the AI sorts and summarises, and a follow-up task is created.

Applications & documents

Parents apply and upload birth certificates and report forms online. Missing documents are flagged automatically. Decisions stay with your team.

Parent communication

Draft replies and announcements in English or Kiswahili in seconds. Send by SMS or email to the whole school, a grade or a class, after approval.

Fee reminders

A weekly batch of polite reminders built from your actual balances. Anyone who has paid, is on a payment plan or was just reminded is skipped automatically.

Built for children's data

Safe by design, not by promise

EduFlow AI was designed around Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. The rules below are enforced by the database itself, so no screen, setting or automation can get around them.

Staff approve every AI message

AI-written text cannot be sent until a named staff member approves it.

Each school's data is sealed

Staff only see their own school, and only what their role needs. A teacher never sees fees.

Financial records are protected

Automations and the AI can never confirm or change a payment. Corrections are reversals, with a reason on record.

Everything is on record

Admission decisions, approvals and payment changes are logged with who did what and when.
